# Projects

A project holds a piece of work and the people doing it. This page covers how they behave day to day.

## Creating and renaming

Create a project from the dashboard with **New project**. The name is the only thing you have to decide, and you can change it whenever you like from the project's settings: renaming does not break links or affect anyone's access.

## Who can see a project

A project is visible to you and to the people you invite to it, and to nobody else. Someone who is not a member cannot open it, cannot find it in search, and does not see it listed anywhere.

If you remove a member, their access stops immediately. The work they contributed stays in the project.

## Archiving and deleting

**Archiving** takes a project out of your active list without touching its contents. Archived projects stay searchable and can be restored at any time. This is what you want when a piece of work is finished but you may need to look back at it.

**Deleting** is permanent. We ask you to confirm, and once it is done the project and its contents are gone for good. If you are unsure, archive instead.

## Limits

How many projects you can keep active depends on your plan. When you reach the limit you can still open and edit everything you have; you just cannot create a new one until you archive something or move up a plan. See [Billing and plans](/docs/billing-and-plans).
