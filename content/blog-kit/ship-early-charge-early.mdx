There is a moment in every side project where it feels almost ready to charge money. That moment is a trap: it renews itself every week, and the redesign that will finally justify a price tag never quite lands.

Charging early changes the questions you ask. A free user tells you what would be nice. A paying user tells you what is worth money, and their refund tells you when you got it wrong. Both signals are louder and earlier than any survey.

This is why the kit wires payments into the free tier instead of treating them as an advanced feature: checkout, customer portal, webhooks and invoices are day-one infrastructure, not a milestone. Put a price on the page this week. You can always change the number; you cannot recover the weeks spent guessing.
